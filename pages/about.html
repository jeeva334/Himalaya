WHO WE ARE

ONE PARTNER FOR COMPLETE BIOMEDICAL SUPPORT.

Himalaya Medical Services was established to provide hospitals with practical, dependable and accountable biomedical engineering support.

Medical equipment management involves more than responding to breakdowns. Hospitals must coordinate preventive maintenance, equipment records, calibration, asset visibility, technical manpower, spare parts, refurbishment and replacement decisions.

HMS helps bring these responsibilities together through structured biomedical support solutions designed around each healthcare organization's requirements.

Our role is not simply to complete a service call. We aim to understand the equipment, the operational requirement and the long-term needs of the hospital before recommending an appropriate solution.
